# Google stitch design

create a design system and core screens for a B2B SaaS learning platform marketplace. Organizations (tenants) host and sell their own video courses; students browse, enroll, and pay per course.

## Part 1 — Design tokens

Typography scale, primary/neutral color palette plus one accent for calls to action, spacing and radius system, shadows.

## Part 2 — Reusable components

Buttons (primary, secondary, disabled), course cards, video thumbnails with duration badge, status badges (active, pending, published), input fields and form states, navigation bar with tenant switcher, avatar/profile menu.

## Part 3 — Student-facing screens

- Homepage: hero with search bar, featured/popular courses grid
- Course catalog: filterable list of all courses
- Course detail page: overview, curriculum broken into modules and lessons, instructor info, price, enroll/buy button
- Lesson page: video player, lesson notes, sidebar with course curriculum, next lesson navigation
- Search results page: ranked result cards showing matched lesson or video moment, linking to the exact timestamp
- Checkout/payment screen for course purchase
- Student dashboard: enrolled courses, progress

## Part 4 — Tenant-facing (admin) screens

- Tenant onboarding / subscription plan selection
- Tenant dashboard: courses overview, revenue summary, student count
- Course builder: create/edit course, add modules and lessons, upload video
- Payout setup screen (connecting a bank account for receiving student payments)
- Payout history / earnings view

Keep the visual language professional and credible — this handles real payments between businesses and their customers, so avoid an overly playful or consumer-social style. Student-facing screens can feel warmer and more inviting; tenant-facing screens should more like business/analytic tool
